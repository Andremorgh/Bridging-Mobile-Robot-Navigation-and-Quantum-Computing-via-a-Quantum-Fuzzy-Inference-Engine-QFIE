# PRISMA Lab (University of Naples Federico II)

<span class="prisma-tag">DIETI · Robotics · Naples</span>

PRISMA Lab (Projects of Industrial and Service Robotics, Mechatronics and Automation) operates within **DIETI** (Department of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology) at the **University of Naples Federico II**.

## Research framing (as stated by the lab)
PRISMA Lab frames its robotics research around four paradigms:

- **Design**
- **Knowledge**
- **Interaction**
- **Impact**

Mission-wise, the emphasis is on making robots *useful and acceptable to humans*.
